Modifications: Poly Pizza GLBs were converted to OBJ+MTL (base colours as Kd; textured palettes baked to per-material
colours; the skinned drone posed at rest). "Man" was cropped to the head, its cigarette removed, rotated to face +Z.
"Man in Suit" was converted from its GLB to Unity space (scaled to 1.8 m), recoloured as a HALCYON security uniform (navy
suit, cyan epaulettes and cuffs, magenta tie), given a baton in the right hand, and four of its clips resampled at 30 fps.
